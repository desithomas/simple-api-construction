The NYC Department of Buildings provides a list of sites across the five boroughs where a complaint was made regarding construction activity that has come to an abrupt halt. When a complaint is made, it typically triggers an official investigation by the New York City Department of Buildings and may result in a official Stop Work Order. Once a Stop Work Order has been put into place, work cannot be continued without the site owner contacting the DOB. Work that continues without the DOB's approval can face fines of up to $8,000 for the first violation and $10,000 for following violations with the potential for criminal charges to be filed.

This list is updated daily and also provided as an Excel workbook. Use this app to check the status of your site and ensure that no complaints have been entered. 

To use this app, enter your sites street address and the borough in which it is located. It will inform you if a complaint was found, the BIN number, and date the complaint was received.
